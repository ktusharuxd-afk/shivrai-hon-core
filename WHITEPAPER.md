## 1. Introduction

SHIVRAI HON (symbol: HON) is a decentralized digital currency built for the people of Bharat. Inspired by the spirit of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, SHIVRAI HON represents financial sovereignty — Swarajya in the digital age.

## 2. Vision

To create a truly decentralized, community-driven digital currency that empowers every citizen of Bharat with financial freedom, transparency, and independence from centralized financial systems.

## 5. Decentralization

SHIVRAI HON runs on a peer-to-peer network with no central authority. Every node in the network validates transactions independently, ensuring:

- No single point of failure
- Censorship resistance
- Transparent transaction history
- Community governance
